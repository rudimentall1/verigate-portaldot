On-chain guard

Execution constraints
The contract is designed to bind authorizations to chain, guard, recipient, exact amount, nonce, deadline and action ID.
Evidence boundary: ALLOW/BLOCK integration was exercised on an isolated local EVM. A full Verigate-authorized payment has not been demonstrated on public PortalDot V3. This prototype is not independently audited and is not a mainnet deployment.
